How to Take a Good Photo

Follow these tips for the most accurate disease detection

✓

Good Photo

  • Single leaf fills the frame
  • Clear and sharp focus
  • Good natural lighting
  • Plain or simple background
  • Affected area clearly visible
✕

Bad Photo

  • Multiple plants in frame
  • Blurry or out of focus
  • Poor lighting or shadows
  • Watermarked stock photo
  • Leaf too small in frame
